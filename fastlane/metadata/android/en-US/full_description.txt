SeQured Identity is a stateless deterministic password manager.

Instead of storing your passwords, the app derives them on demand from four factors you provide:

• A master password
• A 6+ digit PIN
• The site name
• Your username (or email)

The same four inputs always produce the same password, on any device, with no servers involved. A small encrypted vault on the device stores credential metadata + optional TOTP seeds — never the passwords themselves.

Features:

• Stateless derivation via Argon2id + PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA3-256
• Encrypted vault (Argon2id 64 MiB / 3 iters → AES-256-GCM) with automatic upgrade migrations
• Biometric unlock backed by the Android Keystore
• Built-in TOTP authenticator with QR scanner
• Autofill service that derives + fills passwords from any login form (prompt-every-time security model — master never cached)
• Import from Bitwarden, LastPass, 1Password, Chrome, KeePass, generic CSV
• Encrypted export interchangeable with the iOS app
• Zero network calls. No analytics. No tracking. No ads.

Permissions:

• USE_BIOMETRIC — biometric unlock of your PIN
• CAMERA — used solely for the in-app QR scanner that imports otpauth:// TOTP secrets

The full source is at github.com/TC4NS/sequred-identity-android under MPL-2.0.
